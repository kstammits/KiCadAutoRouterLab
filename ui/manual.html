Placement Manual

A short, code-generated guide to the force-spring placement workflow. Every board below is rendered by the current engine; there are no screenshots.

Start here

./start_server.sh
# open http://127.0.0.1:8000

Load a .kicad_pcb board, open the Placement panel, and choose a preset. Step advances the solver and previews the result; Accept writes the previewed pose into the working board. Run repeats the step/accept loop until it settles or hits a limit.

Reading a placement run

SignalMeaning
convergedMotion is below the settle threshold and net force is quiet. Safe to accept.
stalledNo net progress for the stall window, but forces remain. A part is wedged or chattering; move a neighbor or add room.
budgetThe iteration cap was reached while work remained. Treat it like stalled, not settled.
emptyNothing movable or nothing to integrate.
legal / overlapPost-hoc courtyard and board-boundary check on the final merged pose.

Pinned parts and locked footprints are anchors: they apply forces but never move. Ghosts are non-copper anchors — walls that push parts out of keepouts, or attractors that pull a named target.
