Mated pairs (bridges)

Declares that two pads are the two halves of one mated connector (pin header, board-to-board) so the report can see through the mechanical joint. Each link adds a small contact resistance — headers typically 10–20 mΩ, far above a via. Pick fills from the board, Link joins the pair, Suggest proposes mates.
